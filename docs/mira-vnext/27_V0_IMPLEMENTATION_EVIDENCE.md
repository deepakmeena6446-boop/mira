## Product as implemented

MIRA is a mobile movement companion: enter an intention on Go, resolve the places you mean, explicitly choose local departure or arrival time and compare eligible mapped pedestrian routes, assumed duration and calculated daylight. Review the chosen route/map and confirm a private manual journey or a signed-in foreground-location journey with explicitly selected recipients. During movement, reference guidance, manual progress or uploaded-position freshness, check-in, change, immediate support and Emergency remain available. Arrival is distinct from ending early; a return or next leg is reviewed and separately confirmed without rebuilding the other legs. Missing ride/transit coverage leads to navigation/operator/property confirmation and a user-confirmed manual ETA. Longer nights explicitly save the complete plan under the existing encrypted30-day contract; after the default two-hour draft expires, a saved-return picker restores a chosen leg into fresh comparison without automatic start or sharing. This is useful local software; it does not establish live service, open/staffed help, global verified routes or safety outcomes.

## Verdict

The local app now connects intent, meaningful mapped choice, confirmation, active movement and a separate return. It is substantially more than a timer or a collection of navigation cards. All seven have executable local paths and explicit fallbacks. The tested first screen communicates value, and the signature workflow preserves context. **Local implementation demonstrated; full V1 release acceptance remains PARTIAL and public release NOT READY.** Real route/access/service/help outcomes, physical phones and independent operations/privacy evidence cannot be manufactured by polished UI or passing fixtures.
