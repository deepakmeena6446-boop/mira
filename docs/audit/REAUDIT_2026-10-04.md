## 2. Still open

- **Emergency number abroad (RA1 #1).** After a reload abroad, if the new country lookup itself fails, the remembered country from the last 24 h still shows as a one-tap number in the header. The caveat ("if you've crossed a border…") is only in the sheet. To catch the move, Mira would have to store a coarse position, which goes against the "country only, never a position" rule. Owner decision.
- **P11-001:** "kal" (tomorrow) is not understood as a day. **P05-003:** no zone label near now.
- **Not tested, environment limits:** live Google time zones, a real SIGTERM drain, live Claude answers in the browser (the eval below covers the model).
- **About 80 P2s and 96 P3s** from the original report: Hindi build, focus and 320 px layout, multi-leg plans, run brief, Scout and community details.
