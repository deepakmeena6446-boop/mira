# Mira current experience audit

**Scope.** Source and documentation audit at commit 1ae3c01 (29 September 2026). This document makes no production change. Current code, migrations, README and PRINCIPLES take precedence over archived V0 pilot documents and older launch audits. A live mobile walkthrough is recorded below. The working tree already contained unrelated uncommitted companion/API and test changes when this audit began; this design task did not modify them.

## Thesis and decision history

Mira's current promise is to help a woman understand the way before leaving, let only chosen people follow until arrival, and put help one tap away. Its original repeat-use wedge was the “text me when you reach” ritual, improved with a live link that ends on arrival and a missed check-in attempt. The broader [global blueprint](MIRA_GLOBAL_PRODUCT_BLUEPRINT.md) and [launch constitution](docs/launch-ux/02_MIRA_PRODUCT_EXPERIENCE_CONSTITUTION.md) describe a personal safety and real-world intelligence companion. People add objective local observations; Mira combines them with map, place and published evidence, then shows context and uncertainty. Women ground the problem; useful contributions are open to everyone. See [README](README.md) and [principles](PRINCIPLES.md).

The [principles](PRINCIPLES.md) reject neighbourhood safety scores, heatmaps, public individual reports, people-reporting, passive location history and AI-made safety claims. Community is a corroborated sensor network, not an unmoderated feed. Evidence determines truth; AI may prioritise and explain it. These are deliberate product decisions.

The previous [launch UX execution report](docs/launch-ux/FINAL_UX_EXECUTION_REPORT.md) documents a visual-system change, deterministic Mira line, journey hierarchy and easier contribution entry. It retained the full-screen map shell and five tabs. This new problem therefore requires experience architecture rather than another cosmetic pass.

### Data and privacy model

- Accounts, encrypted saved places, Circle contacts, journeys, per-contact links and the final few live points implement chosen sharing. [Account/trip migration](db/migrations/0006_accounts_trips.sql#L3), [trip service](src/server/trips/index.ts#L22). Live points are deleted on close; closed trips are short-lived. There is no route diary. [Trips](src/app/(app)/trips/page.tsx#L20).
- Private reports retain an approximately 1.2 km cell, broad recency/time band and optional encrypted narrative, not the exact submitted point. Approved structured reports are moderator-only; aggregate releases are the public community-note table. [Report migration](db/migrations/0002_reports.sql#L4), [worldwide cells](db/migrations/0006_accounts_trips.sql#L117), [release migration](db/migrations/0001_aggregate_releases.sql#L4).
- Lighting votes and place signals use keyed pseudonymous voter identity. Per-person receipts permit honest impact, with encrypted subject links removed on decision; a Mira Check temporarily holds encrypted journey evidence. [Contribution migration](db/migrations/0013_contributions.sql#L1), [contribution guide](docs/CONTRIBUTIONS.md).
- Mira chat and inbox expire after 30 days. Habits record only an arrived saved destination, mode, departure hour and sharing choice, never a path. [Retention](src/server/retention.ts#L18), [habits](src/server/account/habits.ts#L8).
- Device-local usage events contain action type and local date, capped at 60 events over 60 days; they reorder limited adaptive surfaces and can be reset. [Usage signal](src/lib/usage-signal.ts#L1). The [privacy page](src/app/(app)/privacy/page.tsx#L162) states there are no analytics trackers. No consumer analytics event pipeline was found.
- The service worker caches the shell and static assets, never personal pages, APIs, trip links or invites. [Service worker](public/sw.js#L1).

### Planned rather than built

The [global intelligence and travel paper](MIRA_GLOBAL_INTELLIGENCE_AND_TRAVEL.md) envisages sourced advice cards, destination briefings, itinerary annotation, official/transit feeds and native background support. Current Home supports walk/ride/transit modes and manual ETA, but inspected routes and migrations contain no itinerary model or screen, reusable destination briefing, reviewed airport advice-card store or native background location. [Current mode selector](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L721). Older “future” claims for modes/country profiles are stale; claims that all travel intelligence exists are also stale.

## Current flows

1. **First open:** Anonymous users see two-step Welcome: “With you until you arrive,” four moments including “For everyone,” then a contextual location request. Skip is available. Home is browsable without an account; sign-in gates journeys, Circle/saved places, Mira and durable contribution. [Welcome](src/app/welcome/Welcome.tsx#L22), [Home redirect](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L159).
2. **Daily return:** Home shows map, greeting/area, one adaptive Mira line, destination search and saved places, Circle truth line, Help/Report/Ask, ordinary nearby place list and Safety updates. [Home render](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L561), [Home sheet](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L877). Trips shows active state or mostly empty recent history (closed rows are purged six hours after close). [Trips](src/app/(app)/trips/page.tsx#L20).
3. **Check a place:** Search/pin → destination sheet → travel mode → route/time where available, walking lighting, Help Points, arrival context for ride/transit, sourced city updates and save place. Route failure can fall back to chosen ETA. This is a route sheet, not an independent place-intelligence page. [Destination](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L680), [ETA](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L739).
4. **Contribute:** Street observation tiles, post-journey Mira Checks, structured correction, verified impact and private Mira Scout. Reports can be anonymous; checks/corrections need a durable account for one-person-one-voice. [Contribute](src/app/(app)/contribute/ContributeScreen.tsx#L30), [correction service](src/server/contributions/index.ts#L51).
5. **Feel uncomfortable:** “I feel unsafe” opens immediately from prefetched evidence: Help Point, tell/share, call, Emergency, location in words and Mira last. It distinguishes failed lookup from no mapped point. [UnsafeSheet](src/components/app/UnsafeSheet.tsx#L35), [no-result states](src/components/app/UnsafeSheet.tsx#L135). Trip states who can see the traveller and whether alert attempts can happen. [Trip](src/app/(app)/trip/TripScreen.tsx#L364).
6. **Unfamiliar city:** Search and journey tools depend on geo coverage; country profile localises emergency options; Safety updates are city-level published context. Destination briefing and itinerary do not exist. [Coverage](docs/COUNTRY_COVERAGE.md), [Safety updates](docs/SAFETY_UPDATES.md).

## Reporting, trust and expiry

The report form asks what was noticed, broad recency, experienced/witnessed and optional text. It warns on identifying content. Submission coarsens coordinates to geohash, encrypts text, checks PII/burst patterns and uses an idempotency key. [Report UI](src/app/(app)/report/ReportScreen.tsx#L118), [submit service](src/server/report/submit.ts#L34).

Moderators approve, hold, redact, reject or withdraw for publication/privacy/abuse, not to certify a reporter's truth. [Moderation policy](MODERATION_POLICY.md), [moderation domain](src/domain/moderation.ts#L1). If release is enabled, a weekly note requires five independent approved contributors in one coarse area/time band/category, fresh voices, no burst and a fixed template. It expires after 35 days and can be suppressed. [Aggregation](src/domain/aggregation.ts#L19), [release job](src/server/aggregate/run.ts#L20). A reporter gets a private-submission acknowledgement, not a personal status thread or challenge mechanism. [Thanks](src/app/(app)/report/ReportScreen.tsx#L93).

Condition checks follow another pipeline: at most one question about a Help Point actually passed; open/closed needs two independent people or one agreeing with listed hours. Opposing independent evidence yields “reports differ” and no current impact credit. Place-claim windows are 7–30 days; walker lighting needs three people with 60% agreement inside 90 days. [Check preparation](src/server/contributions/checks.ts#L100), [claim rules](src/domain/contributions.ts#L55). Only verified counted receipts earn impact. Mira Scout is the UI name for Local Steward: durable 30-day account, 25 verified counted answers across 10 days and 3 areas, at least 80% agreement and no anomaly flags. It is private and gives no authority over truth; incident reports never count. [Reputation](src/domain/reputation.ts#L90).

News/official Safety updates are separate from community releases. UI labels publisher, first-indexed age, reporting/allegation status, place precision and independent sources, and says community reports are not shown in beta. [Safety update card](src/components/app/SafetyUpdates.tsx#L195).

## Why it reads as a map product

1. WorldMap is the first visual layer and fills the viewport; information sits in a movable sheet. The map persists without a route or local community evidence. [Home](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L100), [render](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L561).
2. Home's first question is “Where are you going?” Search, saved-place chips, ordinary nearby pins and “Around you” place rows are standard map-app grammar. The Mira line improves wording but not the underlying object model. [Home](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L877), [nearby](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L934).
3. Community has a separate Contribute tab or appears after a journey. Public effects are thresholded and publication is off by default; a new Home can have no visible human intelligence. This is partly a real scale/privacy constraint. [TabBar](src/components/app/TabBar.tsx#L10), [Contribute](src/app/(app)/contribute/ContributeScreen.tsx#L30), [release default](src/server/aggregate/run.ts#L20).
4. A place is mainly a route destination. Mode, ETA, lighting and “Go with Mira” lead; sourced city updates and save-place come later. There is no standalone place-intelligence object. [Destination](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L680), [lower content](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L849).
5. Five permanent tabs give Trips and chat durable status, while verified conditions fold into route/help details. Trips is often empty by design because finished journeys are purged six hours after close. [TabBar](src/components/app/TabBar.tsx#L10), [Trips](src/app/(app)/trips/page.tsx#L20).

**Thirty-second map removal test:** Welcome and Home sheet would still explain a journey companion: destination, chosen people, Help Points and Emergency. They would weakly explain a community-powered local intelligence network. Contribute and occasional adaptive check/impact remain, but little durable evidence shows what people together have learned, especially where release thresholds prevent public notes. Thus **yes for journey companion, no for community-first identity**. [Welcome](src/app/welcome/Welcome.tsx#L48), [Home](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L877).

## What communicates well and what fails

**Works:** first-open makes chosen sharing and privacy explicit; route view states unknown lighting; Trip is honest about followers and alert failures; unsafe help is instant; reporting is coarse/private; checks ask for observed conditions; impact follows verification; country directory does not guess. These are core capabilities regardless of future placement. [Welcome](src/app/welcome/Welcome.tsx#L48), [Trip](src/app/(app)/trip/TripScreen.tsx#L407), [UnsafeSheet](src/components/app/UnsafeSheet.tsx#L35).

**Fails for the broader thesis:** First open then funnels toward destination/map interaction. Community value is explained in prose before demonstrated. The everyday return loop is chiefly repeat journey, one-off check or city news update. A contributor who does not regularly journey with Mira sees little of the local knowledge their work builds. Empty Home says “No detailed places… Search or drop a pin to plan a walk,” reinforcing the map model. [Home empty](src/app/(app)/HomeScreen.tsx#L934), [Contribute](src/app/(app)/contribute/ContributeScreen.tsx#L30). “No recent updates” is caveated correctly, but refers to published news, not community evidence. [SafetyUpdates](src/components/app/SafetyUpdates.tsx#L105).

## Release reality and walkthrough checks

The [public beta gate](docs/PUBLIC_BETA_RELEASE.md) still says **NOT READY FOR DEPLOYMENT AND LAUNCH** pending live providers, staffed moderation, real-phone tests, operations and policy review. Global recognition covers 195 countries, but emergency review and geo/help/lighting/news coverage are uneven. Concepts must avoid implying an active trusted network everywhere.

Live walkthrough should validate at 360–390 px: actual fold balance of map, Mira line and search; location-denied/offline value; dense route evidence versus action; contribution's observation → corroboration → useful local information story; new-user perception without existing releases; and visible safety anchors. JSX cannot prove those outcomes.


## Live mobile walkthrough, added 1 October 2026

I started the current `npm run dev` build on port 3100 and used the repository's installed Playwright/Chromium at **390×844** with a granted location near Delhi North Campus. I inspected `/welcome`, guest Home, `/contribute`, `/report` (including one category form), `/trips`, `/mira` and `/me`. No report was submitted and no external message was sent. The local development backend could not load nearby places or recent updates on this run; those are observed provider failures, not a claim that production lacks those capabilities. I also read the signed-in screen branches and journey flow in code because I did not create or impersonate an account.

- **First open:** `/welcome` is polished and explicit about chosen sharing and location privacy. Its headline is “With you until you arrive”; the four visible moments lead with route, live link and immediate help. “For everyone” is fourth. A first-time user can understand a journey companion, but community is a short explanatory bullet rather than the organising idea.
- **Guest Home:** the map is the full-viewport background. Its exposed region reached roughly **500 of 844 px** before the bottom sheet, with header/help controls over the map; the sheet opens with “Where are you going?” and a place search. Visible map objects and Google attribution made the product read as navigation before any community content appeared. In the sheet, Help/Report/Ask and Around/Safety updates followed below the initial fold. The actual failed-data copy correctly said it could not load/check; it did not claim an empty area was safe.
- **Contribution:** `/contribute` opens to three street/transport/positive-condition report categories and a separate “something that happened” route. Signed-out visitors see a sign-in invitation for checks, corrections and impact. It is a credible private intake, but the first screen is a category directory; it does not show what people together have clarified.
- **Report:** the selected “Dark or broken street” form showed a rough ~1 km area, recency chips, experienced/witnessed choice, optional note and “Send privately.” This is efficient and privacy-conscious. I did **not** submit it. The form is a capability to retain; its placement in the wider product story needs work.
- **Trips, Mira, Me:** guest Trips invites sign-in and describes short-lived journey history; Mira has a chat sign-in gate with prompt chips; Me is a sign-in entry. These are valid secondary capabilities but, as three of five persistent tabs, they offer little community identity to a first-time guest.
- **Existing daily user / place / contributor / unfamiliar city / uneasy person:** those signed-in or destination-dependent live states were not exercised with an authenticated account. The audited code paths above establish their reachable flows and constraints. In particular, the full-map Trip screen is justified by active movement; the same screen treatment on default Home is the perception problem. The current local provider failures also demonstrate why the redesign needs explicit failed and unavailable variants, not just a rich-data mockup.

**Answer to the map removal test after live inspection:** Without the map, the first thirty seconds still say “destination, route, chosen followers, help.” They do **not** demonstrate a durable community knowledge loop. The required change is the default content object and sequence of understanding, not a different basemap or colour palette.

### Discovery rule

A capability belongs on the surface where the person has a matching **intent or state**. Today should establish local knowledge, place intent, personal journey entry, community provenance and an unmistakable Ask Mira entry. The built chat remains a persistent tab after founder review. Place/plan should expose route alternatives, modes, lighting, Help Points and sharing. Live journey should expose ETA, audience and controls. Contribute should expose a verified-impact chart, Scout state, Checks, corrections and reports. You should expose Circle, saved places, habits and privacy. This makes the existing product legible without turning Home into a grid of twenty buttons.
